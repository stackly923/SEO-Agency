DELIVERY PLAYBOOK

Follow a clear release sequence

Use these four steps for every task before closing it in the delivery queue.

  1. Define the acceptance check. Write the observable result before starting implementation.
  2. Review the dependencies. Confirm access, approvals and release timing.
  3. Validate after release. Recheck the live page and record any follow-up.
  4. Close the loop. Summarise the decision and hand over the remaining work.
